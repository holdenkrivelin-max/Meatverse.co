Where It Begins

The MEAT App.

The practical starting point: see your day at a glance, know what comes next, and count the progress you make.

If Life Gets in the Way

The current rough draft keeps unfinished MEATs visible so they can be completed, moved, or set aside deliberately. Accountability without shame.

Not daily minutes spent in MEAT. Daily lives moved by MEAT.

The goal is not to keep people inside another app. It is to help them understand their day, take action, and return to their life.

MEAT helps you remember your life, organize your life, and actually live it.

A Companion For Your Life

The current rough draft organizes Daily MEATs, highlights progress, and keeps unfinished items from being forgotten.

It is being refined through real use before it is placed in more people's hands.

MEAT doesn't need to run your life. It helps you stay connected to it.
