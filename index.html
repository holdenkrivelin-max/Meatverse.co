Inside the App

What are MEATings?

A gentle nudge when you want one.

A MEATing is an optional reminder from MEAT about something you chose to keep on your radar. It may remind you about:

A MEAT waiting for you.
Something moved to MEAT Later.
An upcoming priority.
Something you specifically asked MEAT to remind you about.

"Here are your MEATings for today." That's the whole idea — phone off, on silent, out living your life, MEATings only shows up if you've said you want it, and it only ever asks about what's actually yours.

MEATing: Your electric bill is due tomorrow.
MEATing: You wanted to call Mom after work.
MEATing: One First Step left toward today's goal.
MEATing: Long day? Check in before you plan tomorrow.

MEATings are never mandatory. You choose what MEAT reminds you about, how often, and whether MEATings are on at all.

MEAT works with you, not against you.

Inside the App

What is MEAT Next?

Personalized guidance for what comes next — based on where you are, what you've done, and where you want to go.

MEAT Next is MEAT's personalized "what's next?" layer. After you finish what you already needed to do, MEAT Next helps you decide where to go from there based on your goals, interests, progress, routines, and what matters to you right now.

It can suggest the next step toward a goal, a follow-up from something you completed, an activity that fits your interests, a person or Circle to connect with, a MEATUp or experience, something you've been putting off, or a new direction when you're not sure what to do next. The fourth home in the app, right alongside Today, MEAT Social, and MEAT Rewards.

You handled today. Now let's figure out what's next.

When It's Done, It Moves On

Your day should get lighter as you move through it.

When you complete a MEAT, it leaves your active feed automatically. No clutter. No staring at a growing list of crossed-out tasks.

You complete it.
You earn your MEAT Points.
The moment clears.
MEAT brings forward what's next.

But completed doesn't mean forgotten.

Everything you've finished remains in your history — visually dimmed from active to complete, creating a record of where you've been without competing for your attention now.

Lit up means it still needs you.
Dimmed means you did it.
What's next stays in focus.

Completion creates space.

Not Everything Has To Happen Today

MEAT Later.

Sometimes life changes.

Move a MEAT to MEAT Later and it quietly leaves your active day without disappearing.

Tomorrow, next week, or whenever you're ready, bring it forward again. Nothing is deleted simply because you weren't ready to do it today.

Completed — done and dimmed.
MEAT Later — still yours, just not competing for attention right now.

Nothing lost. Nothing forced. Still yours.

Inside the App

What is MEAT Count?

A visible record of forward movement.

More than a step counter — every meaningful thing you did today, across every part of your life. 3 steps toward fitness, 2 toward starting a business, a bill paid, $50 toward debt. Then MEAT shows both sides: what you did today, and where it's taking you — MEAT Count Today: 7, Financial Goal: 25% complete. Paying a $40 bill counts the same as someone else saving $2,000. MEAT never ranks you against anyone else, only against your own life.

Every step counts. Every payment counts. Every action counts. Because progress should lead somewhere. That somewhere is MEAT.

MEAT Sip

3 seconds. One memory.

A tiny looping moving memory — 3 seconds or less — intentionally different from a normal short-form video.
