WHERE IT BEGINS

One app today. A more connected future.

Begin with one useful promise: help people know what matters and take the next meaningful step. The MEAT Life app is the starting point — MEATVerse is the direction it grows, guided by real use and honest feedback.

Today, MEAT Life is your day planner — what to do, when, and with who. Tomorrow, everything around your plans connects through it: the people you make time for, the places you go, the opportunities that fit your life. One connected direction, built step by step.

If Life Gets in the Way

The app today keeps unfinished MEATs visible so they can be completed, moved, or set aside deliberately. Accountability without shame.

A Companion For Your Life

MEAT Life organizes your Daily MEATs, highlights progress, and keeps unfinished items from being forgotten.

It is being refined through real use before it is placed in more people's hands.

Built Around People

Personal by choice.

MEAT Life App should adapt to people—not ask people to adapt to it. Personalization should be useful, understandable, and optional.
