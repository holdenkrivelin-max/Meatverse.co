Inside the App

What are MEATings?

Your personalized reminders for the day.

MEATings gives you a gentle ping when something in your day needs your attention — a task, bill, goal, appointment, follow-up, First Step, or something you said you wanted to remember.

"Here are your MEATings for today." That's the whole idea — phone off, on silent, out living your life, MEATings only shows up if you've said you want it, and it only ever asks about what's actually yours.

MEATing: Your electric bill is due tomorrow.
MEATing: You wanted to call Mom after work.
MEATing: One First Step left toward today's goal.
MEATing: Long day? Check in before you plan tomorrow.

Not more notifications. Better ones.

Inside the App

What is MEAT Next?

Personalized guidance for what comes next — based on where you are, what you've done, and where you want to go.

MEAT Next is MEAT's personalized "what's next?" layer. After you finish what you already needed to do, MEAT Next helps you decide where to go from there based on your goals, interests, progress, routines, and what matters to you right now.

It can suggest the next step toward a goal, a follow-up from something you completed, an activity that fits your interests, a person or Circle to connect with, a MEATUp or experience, something you've been putting off, or a new direction when you're not sure what to do next. The fourth home in the app, right alongside Today, MEATspace, and MEAT Rewards.

You handled today. Now let's figure out what's next.

Inside the App

What is MEAT Count?

A visible record of forward movement.

More than a step counter — every meaningful thing you did today, across every part of your life. 3 steps toward fitness, 2 toward starting a business, a bill paid, $50 toward debt. Then MEAT shows both sides: what you did today, and where it's taking you — MEAT Count Today: 7, Financial Goal: 25% complete. Paying a $40 bill counts the same as someone else saving $2,000. MEAT never ranks you against anyone else, only against your own life.

Every step counts. Every payment counts. Every action counts. Because progress should lead somewhere. That somewhere is MEAT.

Making Ends MEAT

MEAT Count counts the steps. Making Ends MEAT shows where the financial ones are taking you.

What is MEATspace?

MEATspace is the social layer of MEAT — a place where Meaties can connect around shared interests, goals, progress, and real-life experiences.

See what your friends are working toward, discover people moving in similar directions, join Circles built around what matters to you, support each other's goals, and turn digital connection into real-world action.

MEATspace isn't about popularity. It's about participation, progress, and finding the right people to move forward with. 💚

What are MEAT Circles?

Communities built around what matters to you.

Join Circles based on your interests, goals, activities, location, or what you're working toward. Connect with fellow Meaties, share progress, recommend things to each other, plan MEATUps, and help one another keep moving.
